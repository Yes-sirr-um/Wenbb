01 / The thinking

A degree is only useful if it has somewhere to land.

Most of what I know about business came from small things going slightly wrong: a late delivery, a price that didn't work, an email that got no reply.

Figure 01 · Unknotting execution 01 / Approach

From what a textbook says a business should do, to what actually happens when you run one.

Commerce in school, then a BBA built around digital business and entrepreneurship: the theory is there. What changed things was running small things myself: reselling on Facebook Marketplace, writing affiliate posts, watching what people clicked and what they scrolled past.

The pattern that keeps showing up is that the idea is rarely the hard part. It's the customer reply, the follow-up, the pricing decision, the second email, the unglamorous middle where most of the learning actually happens.
